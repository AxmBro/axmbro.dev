Better Bedrock is a free texture pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, often called a client. It adds HUD mods, a mod menu, redesigned screens, and nearly 300 settings, and it works on PC, mobile, and console. I started the project in 2022, built its JsonUI and textures, and co-built the [official website](https://betterbedrock.com) with iDarkQ. It has passed 2 million downloads, and the latest release is V8.

I also build custom Bedrock UI for servers and studios - see [Commissions](/commissions).
